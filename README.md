⚡ Electric Vehicle Market Analysis | Power BI Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

The Electric Vehicle (EV) industry is experiencing rapid growth driven by environmental awareness, technological advancements, and government incentives. Understanding adoption trends, manufacturer dominance, and regional distribution is critical for policymakers, investors, and automobile manufacturers.

This project analyzes electric vehicle data using Power BI to uncover market growth patterns, vehicle distribution, and consumer preferences. The objective was to convert raw EV data into structured, decision-focused insights through meaningful KPIs and interactive visual storytelling.

🎯 Problem Statement

The goal of this analysis was to evaluate the overall landscape of Electric Vehicles (EVs), including Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs), in order to:

Assess market size and overall growth

Analyze technological advancement through electric range

Identify leading manufacturers and popular models

Examine geographical adoption patterns

Understand the impact of CAFV (Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle) eligibility

📊 Key KPIs

Total Vehicles: 150.42K

Average Electric Range: 67.83 Km

Total BEV Vehicles: 117K (78% of total vehicles)

Total PHEV Vehicles: 34K (22% of total vehicles)

These KPIs provide a quick summary of the EV market structure and the dominance of fully electric vehicles.

📈 Dashboard Insights
1️⃣ Total Vehicles by Model Year (From 2010 Onwards)

A line chart illustrates the growth of EV adoption over time. The data shows a steady increase from 2010, with rapid expansion after 2018, indicating rising consumer demand and stronger policy support.

2️⃣ Total Vehicles by State

A map visualization highlights state-wise distribution of electric vehicles. This helps identify regions with higher EV penetration and potential growth opportunities.

3️⃣ Top 10 Total Vehicles by Make

A bar chart displays the leading EV manufacturers. Tesla leads the market, followed by Nissan and Chevrolet, indicating strong brand dominance and consumer preference.

4️⃣ Total Vehicles by CAFV Eligibility

A donut chart shows the proportion of vehicles eligible for Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle incentives. This analysis helps understand how policy incentives influence adoption trends.

5️⃣ Top 10 Total Vehicles by Model

A treemap visualization highlights the most popular EV models such as Model Y and Model 3, reflecting consumer buying behavior and market trends.
